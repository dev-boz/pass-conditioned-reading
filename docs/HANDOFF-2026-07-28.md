## The M-NL consequence (maintainer, end of session)

**"The prompt only bodes well for a nuance test, since training for that would be a massive
task."** Unpacked: M-NL is untrained-floor, prompt-driven work by design (§8 of the shape doc).
P1e just showed a 7B runs the protocol's structural half from prompts alone — so the coupled
arm of M-NL does not wait on any training run, and a training-for-nuance program (which would
be enormous) is not a prerequisite for testing the architecture's actual claim. This **elevates
M-NL** from "parallel track" to the natural next major move alongside the cheap probe queue.
Its two new pre-registered rules (shape doc §10): **R1** — the rolling-compaction baseline is a
distribution (n rollouts, beat the mean, per-rollout signal-survival audit; purposeful
destruction is a separate stress arm); **R2 (G4)** — before any arms run, the test model must
prove it can make the integrated call from a small clean evidence set; smallest passing model
runs the arms; a G4 scale ladder (1.5B→3B→7B→14B→API) is cheap and diagnostic in itself.
