## The maintainer's call, and what was built on it

The instruction was: the compressor is part of the architecture in its final form, specialised for
the role; hand-edited compressions emulate that; try a general compressor too; try every option and
**mark each run faithful or assisted**; and pace it — one experiment, checked, then the next.

Six arms were built on one identical tiling, so they differ in exactly one thing:

| arm | class | ladder | fill | binding@ | adder@ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `extractive` general deterministic | faithful | 22.5→1.0 | 97% | L6 | L6 |
| `graded` role-specialized deterministic | faithful | 22.0→1.0 | 91% | L2 | L1 |
| `llm_general` Haiku, generic prompt | faithful | 55.7→1.0 | 62% | L6 | L4 |
| `llm_roleaware` Haiku, told its role | assisted | 46.8→1.0 | 47% | L1 | L1 |
| `hand_blind` authored, uniform rule | assisted | 30.5→1.0 | 18% | L1 | L1 |
| `hand_oracle` authored, probe-aware | assisted | 30.5→1.0 | 18% | L1 | L1 |

`faithful` = split-fact guard holds AND views authored probe-blind. `assisted` = otherwise; run and
reported, but measuring retention rather than composition. Stamped mechanically into every manifest.

**Answer to the C1/G5 question:** C1 is now *measured, not asserted* — the runner records the level
at which each operand first survives compression, per arm. C2 became the G5 measurement and stamps
the run instead of aborting it.

## The four results

**1. The specialized compressor had no resolution knob.** `compress_code.py` treats its budget as
advisory and emits a flat **11.8x at every level** — 5,573 tok for the whole document against a
3,000 budget, 347 for a sixteenth. On a pyramid that means `k` indexes nothing, which is the same
defect the D25 schedule had. `src/pca/compress_graded.py` re-expresses it as a sigma ladder
(verbatim → drop bodies → drop prose → drop edges → shape collections → names → inventory), sigma
chosen per module, largest-first. Result: a real ladder at 91% budget fill. The `sigma 0` rung was
added *after* measurement — without it the operator could only tighten, and levels 2-5 pinned flat.
**A compressor that can only remove detail is not a resolution operator.**

**2. The fixture is 97% padding, and that is what made the chain look hard.** Of 106 modules, **six**
declare anything. Their declarations are **1,129 tok**; with a pattern block covering the other 100
that is **1,806 tok = 60% of ONE view budget**. Any compressor that notices the document is a
hundred copies of one module puts the entire distinctive content in the level-1 view. The split-fact
guard survives only where the compressor is bad at salience — the three best arms all break it.
`hand_oracle` and `hand_blind` came out byte-identical: when the compressor is good, a probe-aware
oracle has nothing to add.

**3. The position channel is inert, re-measured where it could have mattered.** P1d on the pyramid,
view/state/rubric pinned, only the label moving: **3/10, 2/10, 3/10, 3/10 across k ∈ {1,8,32,63}**.
Behaviour tracks the view, not the label — the coarse view labelled k=63 emits 16.6 ops with 103
no-ops; the verbatim view labelled k=1 emits 4.5 ops with 1. Edit volume responds cleanly to
resolution (17.4 ops at 22x → 7.0 at verbatim); the label does nothing. **Caveat that matters:** the
student was trained on D25, so it was never trained to use a resolution ladder. This says a
D25-trained student ignores k on a pyramid; it does not say a pyramid-trained one would.

**4. The blocker is retention, not compression or coverage.** Four chain-gate arms, 44 draws:

| arm | ever recorded | at close | destroyed after recording |
|---|---|---|---|
| `extractive` | 0/10 | 0/10 | — |
| `graded` student | 1/10 | 0/10 | 1 of 1 (pass 57, held 19 passes) |
| `graded` **floor** | 0/10 | 0/10 | — |
| `llm_roleaware` | 3/10 | 1/10 | 2 of 3 (passes 7, 17) |

`llm_roleaware`'s level-1 view hands the model the table, the adder and the relation as an explicit
formula, in 1,407 tokens, at pass 1 of 63. Sixty-two passes later: binding 1/10, binding+adder
**0/10**, answer **0/10**. Both clobbers were hand-read — `graded` seed 5's entry S36 held the whole
table at pass 56 and was overwritten at pass 57 by a view containing *only* boilerplate modules.

Across arms the binding entered the state in 4 draws and was gone by the close in **3**. That is
descriptive, not inferential; the between-arm ever-recorded trend (0→1→3, tracking compressor
salience) is monotone but **underpowered** (roleaware vs extractive p=0.211).

**The pyramid's own cost:** doubling the pass count doubles the exposure of everything already
recorded to being overwritten. Seed 5 under D25 recorded at pass 12 and survived; on the pyramid it
recorded at 38 and did not.

## P1h — untested-as-designed, do not read it as a refutation

The provisional-mark mechanism needs a compressor whose coarse views *degrade* the value; only
`llm_general` supplies that (range at L1-L5, exact at L6), and the probe refuses on the others
rather than silently testing an absent precondition. On that arm, **the mark is never emitted: zero
asterisks of any kind across 10 draws**, with the instruction and its worked example verbatim in the
prompt. `fine_confirm_after_prov` (1/10) equals `fine_confirm_after_plain` (1/10), so the mark
cannot be load-bearing — because there were no marks. Retest at a scale that emits it.

## What this means for Phase 2

- **The fixture rebuild has a hard new requirement:** distinctive content must exceed one view
  budget. Otherwise the pyramid's top level answers everything and no schedule can test composition.
  Quantify it with `author_pyramid_views.py --budget-audit` before authoring anything.
- **Edit policy moves up the lever board.** Salience repairs (1a) and binding survival (1b) still
  stand, but neither helps if the entry is overwritten twenty passes later. A no-clobber /
  append-preferring guard is now a first-class candidate, and it is testable at $0.
- **The schedule fix rides along as planned** — but on this evidence it should not be expected to
  move the gate on its own.
